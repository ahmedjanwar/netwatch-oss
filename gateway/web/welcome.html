Welcome to Netwatch 👋

Netwatch turns this machine into a control room for your home network. Here's what it does, in 30 seconds.

🗺️
See every device Phones, TVs, consoles and smart bulbs on a live map, with how much each one uses.
🛡️
Stay in control Block or pause a device, approve new ones before they get online, and block ads for the whole house.
🔒
Private by design Everything stays on this machine. Nothing is sent to anyone, apart from Telegram alerts if you turn them on.
🚦
Starts safe Netwatch begins in monitor-only mode and won't change your network. When you're ready, the setup guide in Settings walks you through gateway mode, which is what blocking needs.
