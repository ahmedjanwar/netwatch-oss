# Code of conduct

Netwatch follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be patient with people who are new to networking or to open source, give
feedback about the work rather than the person, and don't harass anyone. That applies in issues, pull
requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the
[contact form on ahmed-jemal-showcase.vercel.app](https://ahmed-jemal-showcase.vercel.app/#contact).
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block people who
don't follow this code.
